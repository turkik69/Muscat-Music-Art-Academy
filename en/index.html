Arts education with room to grow.

Founded in 2019 in Oman, Muscat Music & Art Academy combines structured learning with artistic exploration for children and adults.

FOUNDER & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

H.E. Dr Sharifa bint Khalfan bin Nasser Al Yahyai

Former Minister of Social Development · Omani academic and researcher

Dr Sharifa has led Muscat Music & Art Academy since its founding in 2019. Her career spans education, research and national service, with a focus on creating opportunities for emerging artistic talent.
